Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Introduction

Emeral ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Emeral mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using Emeral, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Google account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Language preferences — Your native language, target language, and proficiency level.

Information generated through use:

  • Conversation data — Messages you send and receive during practice sessions.
  • Usage data — Daily message count to enforce fair usage limits.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Personalize your language learning experience
  • Enable AI-powered conversation practice
  • Enforce daily usage limits
  • Improve the App over time

AI and Third-Party Services

Emeral uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI — Messages are sent to OpenAI's API to generate AI responses. OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — Profile and conversation data storage. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google OAuth — Sign-in handled by Google. Google Privacy Policy

Your conversation messages are not used to train AI models.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring each user can only access their own data. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Emeral is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
